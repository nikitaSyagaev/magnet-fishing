What is it

A relaxing treasure hunt — with a magnet

Magnet Fishing is a Roblox simulator about magnet fishing: instead of fish you pull up everything people ever dropped into the water. Every world hides its own treasures, every catch makes you richer, and every upgrade takes you deeper.

🧲

Simple to start

One tap to cast, one quick mini-game to hook the catch. You will be fishing within seconds — on a computer or on your phone.

💎

Something new every cast

90 collectible items in five rarities: from a rusty nail to the King's Crown and the Heart of the Abyss. Each season brings its own catch.

🌍

15 worlds to unlock

Ponds and rivers, a sunken ship, frozen lakes, lost Atlantis, an alien ocean, a candy island, a volcano, a pirate cove and more.

How to play

From the first nail to a legendary chest

The whole loop takes a minute to learn and hours to master.

Grab your rod

Take the fishing rod (key 1 on PC or the button at the bottom of the screen) and walk up to the water.

Cast the magnet

Click or tap the water. The magnet flies in an arc and sinks — now wait for the «!» above it.

Win the mini-game

Press when the white marker is inside the green zone. Rarer catches need more hits and faster reflexes.

Sell and upgrade

Sell your catch to the buyer, buy better rods, magnets and a bigger backpack, then open the next world.

💡 Hit the limit? Rebirth for a permanent gold multiplier — your pets, hats, trails and collection stay with you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Magnet Fishing free?

Yes. Magnet Fishing is free to play on Roblox. Game passes are optional and only speed things up.

Where can I play Magnet Fishing?

On PC and Mac through the Roblox website or app, and on Android and iOS phones and tablets through the Roblox app. The controls adapt to touch screens.

How do I catch rare and legendary items?

Upgrade your rod and magnet, unlock new worlds, equip pets that boost luck and fish during the Magnetic Storm, when rare items bite much more often.

What does rebirth do?

Rebirth resets gold, gear, backpack and unlocked worlds, but gives a permanent gold multiplier. Pets, hats, trails, the collection and achievements stay with you. Later worlds require rebirths to open.

Can I trade with other players?

Yes. After 5 rebirths you can trade pets and hats with other players on the same server.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, progress is saved automatically to your Roblox account. While you are away, your net keeps catching junk for you.

Which languages does the game support?

16 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Polish, Italian, Turkish, Indonesian, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai and Vietnamese. The game picks your Roblox language automatically.
